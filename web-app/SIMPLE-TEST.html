🎨 Victoria Coloring Book - DEMO

How to Use:

  1. See the 4 boxes below? Each has a word (CAT, DOG, BIRD, FISH)
  2. See the colored circle in the center? It has 4 colored parts
  3. Watch one part blink! That part is asking you to click it
  4. Click the BLINKING part of the circle
  5. You'll get a message showing which picture you selected!

NOTE: In the full app, clicking opens a fullscreen coloring page. This is just a demo showing the selection mechanism!
