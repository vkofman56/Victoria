## How to Extract (3 Methods):

### Method 1: Screenshot (Easiest)

**Windows:**
1. Open PDF in Adobe Reader or browser
2. Press `Windows + Shift + S` (Snipping Tool)
3. Select JUST the picture (not the word below it)
4. Paste in Paint
5. Save as PNG with the name above

**Mac:**
1. Open PDF in Preview
2. Press `Cmd + Shift + 4`
3. Select JUST the picture
4. Saves automatically as screenshot

### Method 2: Online PDF Converter

1. Go to **https://www.ilovepdf.com/pdf_to_jpg**
2. Upload: `From 1 to 10 with Rhymes 5 pages.pdf`
3. Click "Convert to JPG"
4. Download all pages
5. Open each page in Paint/Photoshop
6. Crop each of the 4 pictures
7. Save with names listed above

### Method 3: Adobe Acrobat

1. Open PDF in Adobe Acrobat
2. Tools → Export PDF → Image → PNG
3. Export all pages
4. Crop each picture individually
5. Save with names above
